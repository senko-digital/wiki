# Contributing to the Senko Digital Wiki

We welcome contributions to improve and expand the Senko Digital wiki/knowledge base. Before contributing, please read the following terms.

---

## 📚 Content Contributions

By submitting written content (articles, explanations, tips, etc.), you agree that:

- Your contributions will be licensed under the **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)** license.
- You grant **Senko Digital LLC** the right to edit, format, and publish your content as a part of the project.
- You will be attributed where appropriate, but we reserve the right to modify contributions for clarity and consistency.
- You will not contribute to self-promote your services. Third-party links may only point to reputable and popular websites.
- You will keep contributions relevant to Senko Digital services and the article's topic.
- If you use AI tools to create or edit your contribution, you must disclose their use in your submission and briefly describe how they were used. You remain responsible for reviewing the content and verifying its accuracy.

---

## 💻 Code Contributions

Currently **we do not** accept public contributions to the code in this repository.

Please do not submit code unless you have received a direct request to do so.

---

## 💬 Questions?

For issues or suggestions, please contact us via one of the contacts listed on our [contacts page](https://senko.digital/contacts).
